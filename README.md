## About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Bioinformatics at CiiM (Joint Center for Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research (HZI) and Hannover Medical School (MHH)), under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Yang Li. I completed my Master's degree in Immunology at Peking University.

## Research

My research focuses on immune regulatory circuitry and high-dimensional omics profiling. I leverage multi-omics technologies and machine learning approaches to decipher the multi-layered regulatory landscapes underlying aging-related immune dysfunction and hepatocellular carcinoma pathogenesis.

**Techniques:** Bulk RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, Bulk ATAC-seq, scATAC-seq, Spatial Transcriptomics, Flow Cytometry, PCR, qPCR, WB, Bacterial Culture, Disease Modeling, and Mouse Management.

## Publications & Service

I have published 7 papers, primarily in Immunology and Bioinformatics, including work in *iMeta*. I have also served as an academic reviewer for *Heliyon* and *Annals of Palliative Medicine*.

## Current Work

I am a member of the sub-project SP12 within the DFG-funded RTG2978 Research Training Group, focusing on the immunomodulatory mechanisms of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
