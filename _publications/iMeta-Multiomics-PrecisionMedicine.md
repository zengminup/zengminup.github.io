Precision medicine aims to deliver individualized disease diagnosis and treatment by incorporating interpatient heterogeneity. The rapid advancement of multi-omics technologies, including genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and microbiomics, has greatly empowered precision medicine by revealing molecular-level variations across patients. In this review, we summarize the framework of multi-omics-driven precision medicine, highlighting how multi-modal omics data integrated with artificial intelligence enables disease subtyping, therapeutic response prediction, and dynamic disease monitoring. We further discuss current challenges including data heterogeneity, batch effects, and translational barriers from omics discoveries to clinical practice, and provide perspectives on future directions. Collectively, multi-omics integration serves as a core engine to advance precision medicine from static molecular profiling toward mechanism-guided, patient-specific clinical decision-making.
