**Welcome To My Academic Homepage**<br/>
I have completed my Master’s degree in **Immunology** at **Peking University**  <img src='images/about/PKU.png' style='width: 6em;'>. I am currently pursuing my PhD in **Bioinformatics** at CiiM (Joint Center for Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research (HZI) and Hannover Medical School (MHH)) <img src='images/about/CiiM.png' style='width: 6em;'> under the supervision of **[Prof. Dr. Yang Li](https://lab-li.ciim-hannover.de/)**  <br/>

My research focuses on **immune regulatory circuitry and high-dimensional omics profiling**. Leveraging multi-omics technologies and machine learning approaches, I aim to decipher the multi-layered regulatory landscapes underlying aging-related immune dysfunction and hepatocellular carcinoma pathogenesis. The techniques I have mastered can be found in the **[Skills](https://zengminup.github.io/skills/)** section (Bioinformatics and Experiment), including **[Bulk RNA-seq](https://zengminup.github.io/skills/bulk-rna-seq)**, **[scRNA-seq](https://zengminup.github.io/skills/sc-rna-seq)**, **[Bulk ATAC-seq](https://zengminup.github.io/skills/bulk-atac-seq)**, **[scATAC-seq](https://zengminup.github.io/posts/blog-sc-atac-seq/)**, **[Spatial Transcriptomics](https://zengminup.github.io/posts/blog-spatial-transcriptomics/)**, and **[Flow Cytometry](https://zengminup.github.io/skills/Genotyping)**, PCR, qPCR, WB, Bacterial Culture, Disease Modeling, and Mouse Management as well as other related techniques.<br/>

During my student career, I have served as an academic reviewer for **Heliyon** and **Annals of Palliative Medicine**. I have published 6 papers, primarily focusing on **Immunology**, and **Bioinformatics**. For example, my work has been published in ***[iMeta](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/imt2.70165)***. Other publications can be found in the **[Publication](https://zengminup.github.io/publications/)** section or **[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=wkBkJnsAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN)** 
[![Citations](https://img.shields.io/badge/Citations-68-blue?logo=google-scholar&logoColor=white&labelColor=blue&color=lightgray)](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=wkBkJnsAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).<br/>

Currently, I work as a member of the sub-project **SP12 within the DFG-funded RTG2978 Research Training Group**, focusing on the immunomodulatory mechanisms of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). My research integrates **multi-omics profiling, chromatin accessibility landscape analysis, and machine learning frameworks** to decipher the layered molecular regulatory networks underlying tumor immune remodeling and aging-related pathological progression, and further develops predictive models for aging stratification and disease progression. In addition, I regularly share my bioinformatics findings and experimental observations in **[Blog Posts](https://zengminup.github.io/year-archive/)**, offering insights into data interpretation and research progress.
